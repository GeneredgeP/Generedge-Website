Frequently asked questions

How does GenerEdge work?

Our network of mission-driven lenders works to bring accessible business financing to underserved markets of minority- and women-owned businesses. You submit one request, and we match it against the lenders in our network whose criteria and field of membership fit your business.

What are the loan terms for alternative business financing?

Alternative lending is often a better match for small business owners than traditional bank lending. Terms are shorter, the application is simpler, repayment is more flexible, and funding is faster. Actual rates and terms are set by the lender, not by GenerEdge.

Do you charge fees and when are they due?

There are no up-front fees. The fee is only payable if and when the loan closes.

Will applying affect my credit score?

No. Getting matched with funding options does not involve a hard credit pull. A lender may run a credit check later in their own underwriting process, and they will tell you before they do.

Is GenerEdge a lender?

No. GenerEdge is a lending connector. We introduce you to third-party lenders and help prepare and guide your file, but approval, rates and terms are determined solely by the lender.
