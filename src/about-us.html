What Sets Us Apart

GenerEdge acts as the borrower’s agent.

We Work for You, Not the Lender

Most brokers charge upfront fees and act as sales agents for high-interest-rate platforms. GenerEdge operates as an independent borrower’s agent — handling even complex capital stack problems with zero upfront fees. We get paid only when you get funded.

Transparency

No hidden fees, no confusing jargon. We keep our communication honest and our online tracking open.

Who We Serve

Our specialty markets.

We specialize in helping Home Improvement Contractors, Homebuilders, Women-Owned and Minority-Owned businesses find low-cost lending sources.

Our Approach

Lower interest rates and approval-ready.

Our portfolio of lenders is an alternative to both traditional banks and online non-bank lenders. GenerEdge connects small business borrowers with a network of credit unions, CDFIs, and community banks that offer lower interest rates and inclusive lending criteria.

Built on the Co-Pilot Principle

You wouldn’t jump from a boat into the ocean or fly an airplane without a buddy at your side. GenerEdge brings that same sense of safety and confidence to business financing. As a community-first team, we don’t just help businesses get funded — we build lasting relationships.
