Frequently asked questions

Is GenerEdge a lender?

No. GenerEdge is a connector. We introduce developers to partners and to third-party lenders, and we help prepare and guide the file, but approval, rates and terms are determined solely by the lender.

What does GenerEdge charge?

There are no up-front fees. Our fee is only payable if and when the loan closes.

What kinds of projects do you work on?

Multi-family developments, large and small. The joint venture form also accepts mixed-use and land-development projects.

What financing can you connect?

Development, construction and permanent financing for multi-family projects, through credit unions, CDFIs and community banks that offer low interest rates.

I have a site but no partner. Can you help?

Yes. That is exactly what the joint-venture program is for. Tell us about the site or the phase, and we look for a developer or builder whose experience fits it.

Where do you work?

We work with developers across the United States, and meet in person in Florida.
